## 4. Attribution and permissions — the framework and the single ask

The honest pattern, applied everywhere: these engagements predate the Oryntaa entity, so the Work section credits them as **"delivered by Oryntaa's founding team"** with a per-project engagement note stating who built it and in what scope. Client-owned metrics (TrackRec's user count, PrepNow's session numbers) are cited as the client's public figures, never restated as Oryntaa outcomes. Nothing implies the company held a contract it didn't hold — this is the version that survives a due-diligence call, and it costs nothing in persuasive power.

What I need back, per project, in one pass:

1. **Permission status** — named publicly with screenshots: yes / no / anonymized. (Espo and MASI additionally per their conditions above.)
2. **Role scope, one honest line each** — e.g., "sole full-stack developer, design to deployment" vs. "frontend within a larger team." TrackRec matters most here.
3. **Year shipped** for the snapshot field, per project.
4. **Metric confirmation** — the exact TrackRec figure the client wants cited, and whether PrepNow's public numbers may be referenced.
5. **Other founders' projects** — anything shipped and showable, especially mobile.

With those answered, all four clean projects get their full grey-structure case studies (Challenge → Approach → Solution → Features → qualitative outcomes) written in the content phase, and the three features get mockup treatments specced in the design phase.
