## Our analytics posture

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics. We do not set tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not build advertising profiles of visitors.

## Essential cookies

We use only the cookies strictly necessary for the site to function and to protect the contact form from abuse. These do not track you across other sites.

## Third-party embeds

If you choose to load the booking calendar or another embedded tool, that third party may set its own cookies under its own policy. These load only when you actively choose to.

## Managing cookies

Because we do not set tracking cookies, there is nothing to opt out of for analytics. You can control or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.
