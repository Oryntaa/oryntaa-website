This Code of Conduct sets out the standards Oryntaa holds itself and its collaborators to. It is placeholder text pending legal review.

## Our standards

We treat clients, collaborators, and each other with respect and honesty. We are direct about what we can and cannot do, we own our mistakes, and we do what we say we will.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, dishonesty, and any conduct that endangers the safety or dignity of others are not tolerated in any Oryntaa space, online or in person.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaches this code, please contact us using the details at the foot of this page. Reports are handled discreetly.

## Enforcement

We take every report seriously and respond proportionately, up to and including ending a working relationship where warranted.

## Contact

Concerns can be sent to the address in the contact block at the foot of this page.
