This Privacy Policy explains how Oryntaa ("we", "us") handles personal information collected through this website. It is placeholder text pending legal review and does not yet constitute a final policy.

## Information we collect

We collect the details you submit through our contact form — your name, email address, and the content of your message, along with any optional company, country, and project details you choose to provide. We do not collect information you do not give us.

## How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry and to communicate with you about a potential engagement. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Analytics and IP handling

We do not store raw IP addresses. Where an IP is used for spam protection, only a salted, one-way hash is retained. Our analytics posture is described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## Data retention

We retain contact submissions for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and our relationship, and delete them on a scheduled basis thereafter. You may request deletion at any time.
