## The challenge

Healthcare admissions interviews are high-stakes and nearly impossible to rehearse for. Every
program — medical, dental, nursing, PA, allied health — asks differently, scores differently, and
gives candidates one shot. Generic mock interviews don't capture that, and human coaching doesn't
scale to the thousands of applicants who need it. The goal was a practice tool realistic enough to
matter, and structured enough that its feedback could be trusted.

## Our approach

We treated this as an AI product problem first and an application second. Before building the
interface, we defined what a "good" interview and a "fair" score meant for each pathway, and made
that the specification the model was held to. The conversation had to feel live — with adaptive
follow-ups, not a fixed script — while the scoring stayed anchored to a school-aware rubric a
candidate could actually learn from.

## The solution

PrepNowAI runs realistic interview rooms with a live AI interviewer that adapts its follow-ups to
each answer, then returns structured, school-aware feedback and a full transcript to review. Around
that core sit the things that make it a real product rather than a demo: subscriptions, free trials,
and billing, so it can serve applicants at scale without a human in the loop for every session.

## Key features

- **Live AI follow-ups** — dynamic, realistic conversations that respond to what the candidate
  actually says.
- **School-aware rubric scoring** — feedback anchored to how each program evaluates, not a generic
  scale.
- **Transcript playback** — every session recorded to review, reflect on, and improve against.
- **Subscriptions & billing** — trials and paid plans built in from the start.
