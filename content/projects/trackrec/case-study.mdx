## The challenge

A salesperson's track record is their most valuable asset and their least portable one. It lives in
CRMs they don't own, in numbers no recruiter can verify, and in claims that every candidate makes
and none can prove. TrackRec set out to turn that record into something a seller owns and a recruiter
can trust — without asking either side to take the other's word for it.

## Our approach

The hard part was never the interface; it was verification. We designed the product around a chain
of trust: sellers describe their performance, and the managers and clients who saw the work
firsthand vouch for it. That turned a résumé bullet into a verifiable claim, and made the data
worth building a hiring workflow around.

## The solution

TrackRec turns sales metrics into verified, recruiter-ready career profiles — deal sizes, quota
attainment, and ICP history — each backed by endorsements from the people who witnessed it. It
shipped as a real SaaS: multi-tenant, with subscriptions and billing, serving both the sellers
building profiles and the recruiters searching them.

## Key features

- **Verified performance profiles** — deal sizes, quota attainment, and ICP history in one place.
- **Endorsement chain** — vouches from managers and clients who saw the work.
- **Recruiter-ready** — profiles built to be searched and trusted by the people hiring.
- **Subscriptions & billing** — a real SaaS foundation, not a prototype.
