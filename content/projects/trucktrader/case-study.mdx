## The challenge

The commercial-vehicle market is broad and unforgiving: trucks, vans, machinery, agri, trailers,
parts — each with its own buyers, its own specs, and its own way of being searched. A marketplace
for it has to make seventeen categories feel like one coherent place, pull in live dealer inventory,
and serve a Benelux audience in more than one language, all while staying fast enough that people
actually browse.

## Our approach

We built for scale and search from the start. Faceted search was the backbone — the thing that turns
a sprawling catalog into something navigable — and everything else was designed to feed it: dealer
inventory feeds so listings stay current, NL-first multilingual routing so the Benelux is served
properly, and a financing path wired straight into individual listings.

## The solution

TruckTrader is a live marketplace spanning seventeen categories with faceted search across the whole
catalog, automated dealer inventory feeds, and per-listing lease financing via a Beequip
integration. Multilingual routing puts the right language first for the region without splitting the
product into separate sites.

## Key features

- **Faceted search across 17 categories** — from trucks to parts, one coherent way to find things.
- **Dealer inventory feeds** — listings that stay current without manual entry.
- **Per-listing lease financing** — a Beequip integration bringing financing to the point of intent.
- **NL-first multilingual routing** — built for the Benelux, not retrofitted for it.
